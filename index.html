Curriculum

12 modules aligned to the Asbestos Abatement Regulations, 2020

View all modules
Module 145 min

Asbestos fundamentals and health risks

Define asbestos and ACMs, why fibres are dangerous, latency of asbestos-related disease, and the safest principle: do not disturb.

Module 260 min

Legal duties and OIZA compliance structure

Employer, worker, AIA, RAC, asbestos removal supervisor and competent person duties under the Asbestos Abatement Regulations, 2020.

Module 345 min

OIZA inventory and site-specific hazards

Walk-through of the OIZA inventory. Primary ACM: ~380 m² of asbestos cement roof sheets at ablution facilities, weathered/damaged/leaking.

Module 460 min

Risk assessment and exposure scenarios

Likelihood × consequence risk rating, with site-specific exposure scenarios for the OIZA ablution facility roof.

Module 560 min

Work classification: non-asbestos, Type 1, 2 and 3

Classifying work as non-asbestos, Type 1 (lower-risk maintenance), Type 2 (notifiable removal), or Type 3 (high-risk friable). OIZA roof removal is Type 2.

Module 690 min

Control of exposure and safe work methods

Wet methods, demarcation, HE-class vacuums, prohibited tools and methods, and the hierarchy of control.

Compliance

This training platform supports awareness and competency tracking. It does not replace site-specific risk assessment, the services of an Approved Inspection Authority (AIA), a Registered Asbestos Contractor (RAC), or qualified legal advice. Training of Type 2/3 work must be delivered by a competent person recognised for asbestos training requirements under the Asbestos Abatement Regulations, 2020.
